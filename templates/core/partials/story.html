The café experience

Room for your
own rhythm.

For a chapter, a fresh idea, or the work that needs a little quiet. Silent Space is a café concept for students, readers, freelancers and remote workers.

Our story
Read & exchange
A mini library and book exchange: read from the shared shelf or bring a book to swap.
Settle in & recharge
The seating concept includes charging points for laptops and phones, with room to study and work.
A little consideration
Keep voices low and phones silent. Give the people around you room to focus.

In the proposal: focus indicator lights (Available, Busy or Do Not Disturb) and a loyalty programme for regular visitors. These remain concept features.
